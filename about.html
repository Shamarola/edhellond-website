02 / The founder

Aleksander S. Ilić

Founder & CEO

Aleksander is an operator and strategist with a career spanning industrial automation, energy and battery systems, and digital infrastructure. edhellond is where that experience meets creative work.

As Head of Operations & Expansion at a Nordic energy-tech scale-up, he headed strategy and growth for a platform linking data centers, renewable assets and industrial sites to national energy markets, driving market entry, strategic partnerships and the rollout of AI-driven optimization across complex, high-value environments.

His background runs through Europe’s battery and energy sector, from lithium-ion R&D to industrial production at scale. He has coordinated multinational teams across five continents and led the team that designed and built an innovation now saving more than €7M a year, after the company scaled it with a second unit based on his design. He knows how to move big projects from idea to impact.

At edhellond he applies the same leadership to brands, creators and local businesses: sharp strategy, decisive execution, and results that last.

Fluent in five languages. Based in Sweden. Working worldwide.
